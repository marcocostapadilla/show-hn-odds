How this works

Each time you open this page, your browser asks the public HN Algolia API for the most recent 1,000 Show HN posts that are at least 48 hours old. Newer posts are left out because their points are still moving. Points are as reported by the API at fetch time. Nothing is stored and there is no tracking.
